Why me?

I do ALL of it

I wear many hats: objects, apparel, packaging, marketing, graphics, tech packs. You name it, I'll get it factory-ready. Roles that demand such a diverse skill set are rare, so this one feels custom-made for me.

Horror games made me who I am

Some of my first games were Slender, FNAF, and P.T. I was there when Dead by Daylight came out, and it became one of my comfort games during the pandemic. Now I want to be on the other side, working from within the Fog.

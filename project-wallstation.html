Wallstation

  • Industrial Design
  • SolidWorks
  • 3D Modeling
  • Product Design

Remote work keeps growing while homes keep shrinking, and a lot of people end up working long hours at desks that just don't fit them. That's rough on backs, knees, shoulders and necks.

The Wallstation is my answer: an all-in-one wall-mounted desk, drawer and monitor stand. The desk and the monitor both slide along the same wall rail, so you can set each one to your height, sitting or standing. I modeled every part in SolidWorks, chose a material and manufacturing process for each one, and documented it all, from the extrusion profiles to the full bill of materials.

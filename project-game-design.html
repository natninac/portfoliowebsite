Game Design Education Software

2026

  • UX/UI Design
  • Game Design

Professor Pippin Barr is writing a book on learning game design by making variations of existing games. My classmates and I created an edutainment software that teaches game design through modification of simple known games.

Values

Through research and conversations with Barr, we set radical experimentation and play as the software’s core values, and designed every affordance to reflect them. Our core audience is students in higher education learning game design.

Aesthetic

The visuals draw on Frutiger Aero, an aesthetic our generation feels nostalgic for. In its time it stood for technological optimism, which matched our design values.

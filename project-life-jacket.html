Life Jacket

2026

  • 3D Modeling
  • Product Design

This model was created in the context of a 3D modeling Blender class. We were tasked with creating two versions of an "everyday object": one realistic, the other stylized. I chose the life jacket and created a realistic version that has heavy wear and tear, and a stylized version that is lighter, more colorful, and has a surrealist aesthetic.
